How this works

Doge Growth places a puppy's weigh-ins on veterinary growth standards, projects its adult weight and estimates daily energy. It reports estimates and their spread, not feeding or veterinary advice.

Growth chart

The lines are centiles: of 100 healthy dogs of the same sex and size, about 2 weigh less than the 2nd centile, 50 less than the 50th and 98 less than the 98th.

  • Data: 202,334 weights of 52,868 healthy purebred dogs in normal body condition (the open Banfield clinic data behind the WALTHAM puppy charts), refitted by sex for six adult-size groups, 8 weeks to 2 years.
  • Breed: a breed or an expected adult weight sets the chart between the size groups at that weight. Breed weights come from breed standards adjusted to clinic dogs; a typed weight replaces them. In test dogs not used for fitting, this cut the median error of predicting the weight between 1 and 2 years from one weigh-in at 8–16 weeks from 10.6% to 9.8%; the gain is smaller when the breed weight is off.

Projection

The projection carries the puppy's centile forward, drifting toward the median as real puppies do. The shaded bands are the 50% and 90% ranges of likely weights and narrow with each weigh-in. In test dogs not used for fitting, they held 64% and 92% of the next weigh-ins.

Warnings

A note appears under the weigh-ins when the latest weight is below the 2nd or above the 98th centile; when, since the first weigh-in, weight has crossed more than two centile lines (fewer than 1 in 10 healthy puppies do: 3% in research colonies, 8% in clinic records); or when it has not risen over 3 weeks or more in which a puppy staying on its centile gains over 5%.

Daily energy

Metabolisable energy per day, from body weight (BW, kg) and, for puppies, the share of adult weight reached (p). FEDIAF 2025 gives the main estimate; NRC 2006 and AAHA 2021 are shown for comparison.

RER (resting energy requirement) = 70 × BWto the power 0.75. Puppy equations apply from 8 weeks until the dog is both 1 year old and at 95% of its adult weight, and up to 2 years at most. In studies of early growth, measured intake was about 15–30% below the NRC 2006 equation, and individual needs can differ from these averages by up to about 50%.

Food per day is the energy left after the treat share, divided by the food's energy from its label.

Table download

Once a weigh-in is charted, the download button in the top bar saves a CSV of the key figures, the weigh-ins and a monthly projection with energy and food per day.

Limits

  • Charts cover 8 weeks to 2 years.
  • The data are US purebred dogs. For mixed breeds, and for dogs expected to weigh under about 3.5 kg or over about 60 kg as adults (the range of the breeds in the data), the charts are approximations; the chart for 40 kg and over is provisional.
  • Projections assume healthy growth continues.
